Built-in German, French and Spanish texts next to English and Turkish (`labelsDe`, `labelsFr`, `labelsEs`, `resolveLabels(locale)`), with singular/plural forms and locale digit grouping (fixes "1 rows"). Import problems carry a stable `code` and `type`, and `importRows` takes `locale` and `messages`.
